Erhu - Version: 1.0 Name: Esko Ilola ## Included formats - Decent Sampler The erhu is a traditional two-stringed bowed instrument from China, often called the “Chinese violin.” It has a small hexagonal or cylindrical soundbox, usually covered with python skin, and a long,. The bow is fixed between the two strings, allowing for smooth, expressive playing with a wide range of tones—from soft and lyrical to bright and piercing. The erhu is widely used in Chinese folk music, opera, and contemporary compositions, known for its ability to closely mimic the human voice.
