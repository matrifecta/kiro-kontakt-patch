===== 1781 - Ganer Square Piano ( item-13|ds )
RAW: Hi everyone! I am glad to release the two 18th-century British pianos - The 1796 Broadwood Baby grand and the 1781 Ganer square piano virtual libraries for decent sampler via Pianobook, developed for my Postgraduate (taught) Project. The samples were recorded inside the anechoic chamber at ISVR, University of Southampton, United Kingdom, where the Professor, musician and composer David Owen Norris contributed to the recordings, which were later used for physics-based modelling and comparisons. It is a collaborative project between the Institute of Sound and Vibration Research and the University of Southampton's Music Department. There are three variations for Broadwood (pedals): > No Pedals (F0 - key switches), > with Una Corda Pedal (G0), > with Sustain Pedal (A0) and there are 8 variations for the Ganer square piano (the dampers and buff stops): > No dampers; no buff stop (C0), > No dampers with.

===== Austrian Jaw Harp ( item-65|ds )
RAW: The Jaw Harp, also called the Juice Harp, Mouth Harp, or the Jew's Harp is a fun one note instrument. By striking a netal tong between two metal extrusions framing either side of the tong, and placing the frame against your teeth, you can generate a tone that is amplified by your open mouth. By changing the position of your lips and toungue you can further change the tone of the note. This VST I have created, while simple, is still a lot of fun and is silly. Hopefully you all enjoy it as much as I do!
